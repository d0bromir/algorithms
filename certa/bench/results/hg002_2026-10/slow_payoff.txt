# Payoff of the techniques taken from the slow mappers (tools/payoff, bench/payoff_union.py), galaxy, 64 threads, 2026-10-22, build ea812d8 + tools/payoff.
# Each technique certifies the minimum end-to-end edit distance of a read (both strands) by pigeonhole over its parts, on the 2 M HG002 reads (single-end) CERTA leaves uncertified:
#   fixed  CERTA's parts (support 1 at budget 4096, every hit verified on its own band); rare  the most disjoint 22-mers (15-mers) with the fewest hits (DP);
#   long   exact parts of any length (counted through a q-mer inside them, extension <= 40 each side, q-mers with > 20,000 hits skipped);
#   err1   disjoint q-mers each searched with up to one edit (all one-edit neighbours looked up; radius 2|S| - 1); err1r  the same for the rarest disjoint q-mers only.
# Budget 4096 hits per strand (pass 3's), radius cap 15. "vs truth": Yara 1.0.5 -e 4 -y full (6 edits) or -e 8 (12 edits) on the same reads; "truth fewer edits" would mean a wrong certificate.
# "CPU vs CERTA": the cascade's CPU against CERTA's for all 2 M reads in interval mode (197.2 s with --full-cert --index2, 104.6 s with --full-cert; slow_galaxy.tsv). Prototype costs (scalar band DP per hit, per-read lookups).
