For scale (final_*.tsv): BWA-MEM2 110.6 / 256.5 s and minibwa 36.9 / 79.4 s
on a2; Yara -e 4 -y full 138 / 211 s at 2 M on galaxy (slow_galaxy.tsv).

- DRAGMAP (Illumina's open-source re-implementation of the DRAGEN mapper,
  the clinical standard) runs at BWA-MEM2's speed single-end and 1.6x
  slower paired on x86-64; CERTA's interval mode is 5.7x and 11x faster.
  Its aarch64 build is 4-5x slower again (probably because its vector
  code targets x86-64 only).
- Columba (lossless, search schemes over a bidirectional FM-index, dynamic
  partitioning) is the fastest lossless mapper measured: 11x faster than
  Yara single-end. CERTA --full-cert is 2.2x faster single-end, 4.6x paired,
  at about twice Columba's memory (an index of every q-mer position against
  an FM-index).
- mm2-fast is no faster than minimap2 for short reads (its AVX-512 work is
  in long-read chaining and extension); minimap2 2.31 is the faster of the
  three.

Single-end, Columba is lossless as it claims: none of the reads CERTA
certifies within its 6 edits is missed, none placed elsewhere with more
edits than the proven minimum (the 462 "missed better" are equal-edit loci
that score lower under bwa-mem's affine scheme). The 17,832 reads it leaves
unmapped all have a proven optimum beyond 6 edits; CERTA certifies them.
Paired, it trades per-mate optimality for pairing: 0.56 % of reads placed
elsewhere with more edits than the proven minimum, 7,815 left unmapped
within its rate.

== Not run
- NVIDIA Parabricks 4.8.0-1 fq2bam: its arm64 build stops with SIGILL on
  galaxy's Ampere Altra (ARMv8.2; the build targets Grace, ARMv9), and a2
  has no GPU. The GPU baseline needs an x86-64 or Grace host with a GPU.
- mapquik: needs a 2023 nightly Rust toolchain (and its hashing crate is
  x86-only); not installed.
- DRAGEN and Sentieon: commercial licences; DRAGMAP stands in for DRAGEN.
- strobealign with multi-context seeds: already in the benchmark
  (strobealign 0.18.0, --mcs always by default).
