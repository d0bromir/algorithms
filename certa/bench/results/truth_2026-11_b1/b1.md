# B1 — the lift-and-commit ceiling (offline, HG002 test B)

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Step:** Pillar B (reference content), B1 ceiling —
measure before implementing, as A1 did for Pillar A. **Code:**
[`../../b1_ceiling.py`](../../b1_ceiling.py). Raw output:
[`b1_ceiling.md`](b1_ceiling.md).

## Question

Pillar A (the phantom prior) makes CERTA *abstain* on missing-copy reads.
Pillar B should *place* them correctly by adding the missing reference content.
B1 asks: of CERTA's confident GRCh38 misplacements, how many would a
**CHM13 lift-and-commit** place at the truth (rescue), abstain on, or still
misplace? Uses existing scored npz — `certa.npz` (CERTA on GRCh38), `t2t.npz`
(BWA-MEM2 on T2T-CHM13 hs1, lifted to GRCh38 via `hs1ToHg38`, `headroom/t2t.sh`),
`truth.npz` — no mapper change, no new data, full test B (31.9 M mates).

## Result

**CERTA confident (MAPQ≥40) GRCh38 misplacements: 4,923**

| CHM13 lift outcome | all | in segdup | outside segdup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **at truth (RESCUE)** | **1,916 (38.9%)** | 1,239 (39.1%) | 677 (38.7%) |
| abstains (not lifted) | 407 (8.3%) | 125 (3.9%) | 282 (16.1%) |
| still misplaced | 2,600 (52.8%) | 1,808 (57.0%) | 792 (45.2%) |

- **Missing-copy class** (10,899 mates with no GRCh38 truth): CERTA confidently
  places 2,752 (all errors); the CHM13 lift abstains on 405 of those (correct).
- **Overall:** the CHM13 lift *alone* is **worse** than CERTA GRCh38 — at-truth
  99.545% vs 99.861%, confident errors **32,596 vs 4,923** (BWA-MEM2 on CHM13
  introduces many errors of its own).

## Interpretation

- **The lift rescues ~39% of CERTA's confident errors** — places them at the
  truth, which the phantom prior only abstains on. This is the concrete Pillar B
  dividend over Pillar A (matches the report's "BWA-MEM2 on CHM13 rescues 38.3%
  of no-GRCh38-run mates"). Combined with the phantom (abstain on the rest), a
  selective lift-and-commit approaches the confidence ceiling.
- **But the lift must be SELECTIVE.** Committing every CHM13 lift would replace
  4,923 confident errors with 32,596 — far worse. levioSAM2's
  **commit / defer / suppress** reconciliation is essential: commit the lift only
  when it is confident *and* better than the GRCh38 placement; otherwise defer to
  GRCh38 (with the phantom abstention) or suppress.
- The rescue is uniform across segdup/non-segdup (~39%), so the benefit is not
  confined to the segdup track — it is the missing-copy class broadly.

## B1 implementation design (next step)

Selective lift-and-commit in CERTA's `--t2t` path (which already maps CERTA on
CHM13 and lifts via `chain.cpp`, currently features-only):
1. Use **CERTA-on-CHM13** certified opinions (not BWA-MEM2) — the certificate
   gives a sound basis for "confident".
2. **Commit** the lifted GRCh38 placement when the CHM13 placement is
   certified-unique/high-MAPQ *and* reconciles better than the GRCh38 placement
   (lower edit distance / higher score); report it in GRCh38 coordinates with a
   lifted-origin tag. The certificate becomes a statement over the lifted locus.
3. **Defer** to the GRCh38 placement otherwise (phantom prior abstains on
   missing-copy loci); **suppress** source-only (unliftable) regions.
4. Validate per-locus (FixItFelix's FANCD2 lesson: a decoy/lift can steal real
   reads); freeze on HG002, hold out HG005.
5. Data: hs1 v1.1 + `hs1ToHg38` chain are on galaxy; upgrade to **CHM13 v2.0 +
   the nf-LO `chm13v2-grch38.chain`** (better SD lift) is a follow-up download.

## Go / no-go

- **B1 justified:** a selective lift-and-commit can rescue ~39% of the confident
  errors that Pillar A abstains on — placing them correctly rather than merely
  abstaining. Proceed to **B1-impl** (selective commit/defer/suppress in
  `--t2t`), then B2 (one-pass sinks) for the unliftable fraction.
