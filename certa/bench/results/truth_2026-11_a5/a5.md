# A5 — calibrate the phantom prior on HG002, freeze, validate on HG005

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Step:** research guideline Pillar A, A5 (leakage-free
calibration; calibrate-then-freeze). **Code:** [`../../fit_a5.py`](../../fit_a5.py),
[`../../a5_run.sh`](../../a5_run.sh). Results: [`a5_fit.md`](a5_fit.md),
[`a5_holdout.md`](a5_holdout.md), frozen params [`a5_params.json`](a5_params.json).

## Protocol

Sweep δ ∈ {0.003, 0.006, 0.01} × π_∅ ∈ {0.003, 0.01, 0.03} (π_∅_unique=0, GIAB
segdup track) on the **HG002** 4 M single-end subsample; score each by
**calibration error** `Σ_bands |misplaced − expected|` (expected = `Σ
10^(−MAPQ/10)`), which penalizes both overconfidence and over-abstention.
**Freeze** the best, then run it once on the held-out **HG005** 4 M subsample
(a different sample — the true holdout) vs the HG005 `--bio` baseline. No HG005
data is used for fitting. (δ≈0.003 targets the measured ~2 Phred/mismatch slope.)

| 0.003 | 0.003 | 1,766 | 135.1 | 54.9 | 190 |
| 0.003 | 0.01 | 1,773 | 134.5 | 54.9 | 190 |
| 0.006 | 0.01 | 1,804 | 129.0 | 54.9 | 190 |
| 0.01 | 0.003 | 1,903 | 135.7 | 54.9 | 190 |
| 0.01 | 0.01 | 2,039 | 134.5 | 54.9 | 190 |
| 0.01 | 0.03 | 2,956 | 128.7 | 54.9 | 190 |
| 0.006 | 0.03 | 3,281 | 145.7 | 54.9 | 190 |

**Frozen: δ=0.006, π_∅=0.003, π_∅_unique=0.** Larger π_∅ (0.03) over-abstains
(calibration error rises ~2×); the MAPQ≥40 error count plateaus at 190 across
the grid (the locus prior, not the exact δ/π_∅, sets that floor). δ=0.003–0.006
is the calibrated slope region.

## Holdout (HG005, frozen params)

| run | MAPQ≥60 mispl. | MAPQ≥40 mispl. | MAPQ≥20 mispl. |
|---|---|---|---|
| HG005 `--bio` | 264 | 351 | 745 |
| HG005 frozen phantom | **138** | **180** | **634** |

- **MAPQ≥40 errors −49% (351 → 180)**, MAPQ≥60 −48% (264 → 138), MAPQ≥20 −15%
  (745 → 634). **Placements identical** (at-truth 10.045%, 2,539 misplaced) —
  certificate untouched.
- **Strata (MAPQ≥20):** segdup 545 → **444 (−19%)**, lowmap_segdup 644 → 543
  (−16%), difficult 686 → 585 (−15%); **easy 5 → 5 (unchanged)**.
- **Calibration transfers:** the MAPQ 50–60 band's overconfidence falls from
  288/3.0 ≈ 96× (baseline) to 155/2.9 ≈ 53× (frozen) on HG005 — the HG002 fit
  generalizes to a different sample (no overfit).
- Missing-copy class ("no linear truth"): MAPQ≥20 169 → 150 (−11%).

## Interpretation

- The calibrate-then-freeze protocol works: parameters fit on HG002 by
  calibration error reduce confident errors ~49% on the held-out HG005, with the
  certificate intact and easy reads unaffected. This is the leakage-free
  validation the guideline requires.
- The single-end phantom prior is now **calibrated and cross-sample validated**.
  Combined with A4's locus specificity, the principled posterior term delivers a
  robust ~2× confident-error reduction (A1's fitted gap map showed up to ~14× by
  abstaining more aggressively; the phantom term trades less sensitivity).

## Go / no-go

- **A5 validated** (keep the frozen δ=0.006, π_∅=0.003 as the calibrated default
  for the segdup track).
- **Remaining for production:** extend the phantom term to `bio_pair` /
  `bio_rescue` so the **paired** MAPQ benefits (currently single-end only); a
  sharper copy-deficit track (Aganezov collapsed loci / CHM13 copy-excess) and a
  fitted π_∅_unique>0 could refine further. Conformal calibration (roadmap §2.4)
  remains an optional distribution-free guarantee.
