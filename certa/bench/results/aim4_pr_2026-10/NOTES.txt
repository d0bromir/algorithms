Files:

  check_hg002_2M_first_version.log    the first version of the pair check

The first version bounded the pairs through a chain of mate x by the best
x in the chain's band plus the best y anywhere in its partner window. It
left 24 HG002 records (12 pairs) unproven. A debug harness gave the
failing chain for each, and an exact oracle (per-start bests of both
mates over the region, paired under the proper-pair constraint, the shown
pair excluded) gave the best other proper pair there:
  - 7 pairs hit the DP-cell cap (pairs in high-copy repeats, 450-1,600
    part occurrences, every chain searched);
  - 5 pairs failed a chain where the exact best other proper pair was
    below XS (e.g. XS 253: a chain value of 270 combined an x 663 bp from
    the shown one with the shown partner about 1,000 bp from that x; the
    exact best other pair there is 245; XS 275: chain 281, exact 153).
  The mapper's S2 held in every case. The fix: pair x's and y's per-start
  maxima exactly (every pair counted is then a real proper pair), and skip
  a chain when its bound plus a bound on y there (U_t(y), or the bound of
  a chain of y whose start range meets the window) cannot exceed XS.
  With it, all 4 M records of each sample are accepted.
