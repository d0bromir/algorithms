# A3 — `--bio-phantom`: a phantom-copy term in the `--bio` posterior (HG002 test B)

**Date:** 2026-11-21. **Step:** research guideline Pillar A, A3b (phantom-copy
posterior; the novel term from the theoretical-limits doc, Limit 5).
**Code:** `include/certa/bio.h` (`BioParams::phantom_*`, `bio_detail::phantom_weight`,
`bio_mm_counts`, `bio_place`), `src/map_config.cpp` (`--bio-phantom/-pi/-err`),
`tests/test_certa.cpp`. Raw table: [`a3_report.md`](a3_report.md). Runners:
[`../../build_a3.sh`](../../build_a3.sh), [`../../eval_a3.sh`](../../eval_a3.sh).

## Change

The `--bio` posterior is `MAPQ = min(60, 10·log10((1+Ω)/Ω))` with
`Ω = w(yh−p1) + Σ w(pen_i−p1)` — conditioned on GRCh38's competitors, so a
unique horizon-limited read saturates at 60 regardless of its divergence
(Limit 2). A3 adds an **unseen-paralog hypothesis** to `Ω`: a copy at
divergence δ (`--bio-phantom`) from the best hit, prior π_∅ (`--bio-phantom-pi`),
with relative likelihood `(δ/(snv+err))^k · ((1−δ)/((1−snv)(1−err)))^matches`,
`k` = mismatch count from the traced CIGAR (`bio_mm_counts`: `nm − indel bases`).
A read whose divergence looks like a missing copy now abstains. **MAPQ only**:
the term is added after the ML optimum is fixed, so the certificate is
unchanged; `phantom_delta=0` (default) ⇒ byte-identical. Single-end `bio_place`
posterior first; `bio_pair`/`bio_rescue` (paired production) are follow-ups.

## Correctness (galaxy ARM64, RAM build)

- `verify_lemmas` + `test_certa` **pass** (all certificate checks; FAIL=0).
- New unit tests: weight 0 by default; grows with divergence; a perfect read
  stays MAPQ ≥ 55 while an 8-mismatch read drops < 30; `bio_mm_counts` correct.
  Measured: **weight 2.99e-7 (0 mm) → MAPQ 60; 0.125 (8 mm) → MAPQ 10**.
- Real data: **placements identical** (at-truth 10.017%, 2,654 misplaced both
  runs) — the certificate is untouched at scale.

## Results (4 M-read single-end subsample of test B mate 0; δ=0.01, π_∅=1e-6)

| run | placed (mate 0) | MAPQ≥60 placed / misplaced | MAPQ≥40 misplaced | MAPQ≥50 misplaced |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--bio` | 3,201,304 | 3,061,069 / **358** | 461 | 393 |
| `--bio-phantom` | 3,201,304 | 2,635,545 / **280** | 460 | 390 |

- **MAPQ-60 errors −22% (358 → 280)**: ~425 k reads demoted out of the top band
  (mostly to MAPQ 50–58), of which 78 were misplaced.
- MAPQ≥40 errors essentially unchanged (461 → 460): with π_∅=1e-6 the term
  demotes k=1–3 mismatch reads to 44–58 (still ≥40); only k≥4 reads fall below
  40, and few confident errors are unique k≥4 reads.
- Calibration: the MAPQ 50–60 band remains overconfident (393 vs 3.2 expected →
  390 vs 3.5) — the phantom shifts reads down a band but does not by itself
  recalibrate the posterior.

## Interpretation

- The mechanism is correct and certificate-safe: divergent unique reads abstain
  instead of saturating, exactly the Limit-2/5 fix. The top-confidence error
  rate drops 22% with no placement change.
- **The effect is parameter-dependent and modest at π_∅=1e-6.** π_∅ is a global
  prior here; the report's design makes it *locus-specific* (copy-deficit from
  CHM13/HPRC/GIAB strata) so missing-copy loci get a large π_∅ while unique loci
  get ~0 — recovering sensitivity. That is **A4** (locus prior). The optimal
  δ, π_∅ (and the divergence cap A3c) must be **fit on HG002** (**A5**) to
  reproduce the measured ~2 Phred/mismatch slope and demote the missing-copy
  errors below 40 without over-abstaining.
- A1's 14× came from a fitted gap→error map over all mates; A3 supplies the
  principled posterior term, A4/A5 calibrate it.

## Go / no-go

- **A3 mechanism validated** (keep; flag-gated, default byte-identical).
- **Proceed to A4** (locus-specific π_∅) **and A5** (fit δ/π_∅ on HG002, freeze,
  HG005 holdout), then **extend the term to `bio_pair`/`bio_rescue`** for the
  paired production MAPQ. A3c (divergence cap) needs per-locus mismatch plumbing
  beyond `bio_mm_counts` and is deferred.
